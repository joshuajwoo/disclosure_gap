# Predictor/outcome overlap audit

Status: complete for contract version 1.0.0. Repeat after any question or instrument-version change.

The APA SAD-D Adult outcome covers acute fear, general social anxiety, negative-evaluation thoughts, physical arousal, tension, social avoidance/escape, overpreparation, distraction, and reliance on coping aids. The audit uses the official `SAD01`–`SAD10` order without reproducing item wording.

The primary intention–action gap contains no symptom, fear, or diagnostic wording. It still has conceptual proximity to the outcome’s general avoidance items, so the report must describe this as construct proximity rather than claim complete independence. No predictor will be selected or rewritten after access to real follow-up outcomes.

Two protocol reviewers must confirm this mapping during the institutional submission. That procedural confirmation does not reopen the prespecified analysis unless the reviewing body requires a change.
