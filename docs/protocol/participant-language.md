# Participant-language guide

## Required framing

- “Choosing whether to share is personal. Keeping something private can be healthy.”
- “These summaries describe only the answers you provided.”
- “This study does not diagnose social anxiety or tell you whether you should share.”
- “You may skip optional questions or stop participating.”

## Prohibited framing

Do not use “risk,” “warning sign,” “unhealthy secrecy,” “failure to disclose,” “detected anxiety,” “normal/abnormal,” or statements that imply sharing is preferred. Do not show diagnostic labels, instrument cutoffs, predicted outcomes, treatment advice, peer rankings, or cohort comparisons.

## Validation and error messages

Say what is needed without blame: “Choose an option to continue, or select ‘Prefer not to answer.’” For schedule conflicts: “This check-in is not available right now.” Never reveal whether another participant ID exists. Deletion confirmation must state scope and timing based on the approved retention policy.

## Trends

Use counts and neutral descriptions, including missing denominators: “In 2 of 3 check-ins where you wanted to talk with someone, you reported talking with them.” If there are no eligible weeks: “There is not enough information to summarize this pattern.” Do not interpret why a pattern occurred.

## Distress and support

Consent must explain that the service is not monitored for urgent needs and collects no free-text requests. Any approved resource link must be general, geographically appropriate, reviewed by the institution, and not triggered by an individual score.
